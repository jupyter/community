An initiative to evaluate and improve how Jupyter and its subprojects welcome new
contributors.


## In scope

These are things we _may_ do under this initiative:

* Gathering feedback from community members
* Mapping & improving navigation across the community & subprojects
* Evaluate and improve barriers to diverse community participation
* Discovering, sharing, and creating educational resources
    * How to roadmap
    * Communication & social skills
    * Managing quantity and quality of attention in the AI era
* Discovering and sharing examples of well-done welcome mats in other projects
* Creating a welcome mat for the community committee and/or this initiative
* Recommending best practices
* Applying best practices within Project Jupyter & subprojects
