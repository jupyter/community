# Community calls

Virtual events to communicate between subprojects and with the Jupyter user community as a whole.

Please reference the
[Jupyter community calendar](https://docs.jupyter.org/en/stable/community/content-community.html)
for details on attending community calls.

Community calls are also
[announced ahead of time in the Jupyter Zulip](https://jupyter.zulipchat.com/#narrow/channel/508280-Events/topic/Community.20Call/with/522675606).
Please join us there to keep up to date on this and other events!
